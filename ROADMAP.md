# Roadmap

## Foundation — implemented

Pinned toolchain, schedule rules and timing core, presentation text, persistence, live refresh,
and the live menu bar panel. Local/CI validation results are evidence, not completion of manual QA.

## Standalone MVP — implemented, verification next

The 24-hour dial with multiple sessions, day plans, the settings window (Today, Regular Schedule,
General, About), and opt-in login launch are implemented.

1. Complete the [timer manual guide](testing/MANUAL%20TO-DO/timer.md), accessibility checks,
   and a distribution decision before calling the app ready for users.

## Optional connections — after MVP

1. Agree on a small read-only Slopometer progress/goal/freshness contract and a local transport.
2. Add goal pacing beneath the timer; define scope alignment and tolerance before calculating it.
3. Evaluate Google Tasks and other providers, then add selected-list task summaries.

These milestones are proposed sequence, not delivery dates. Research belongs in a research
document when there is actual evidence to preserve; historical superseded documents can move to
an archive when needed. Neither overrides the active scope or implemented logic contracts.
